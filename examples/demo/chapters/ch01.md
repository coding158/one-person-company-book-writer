# Chapter 1 The First Task

Everything in this demo is fictional. On a Monday morning the author asked one AI assistant to draft a checklist and a second one to review it. [ev: 1a2b3c4]

## What happened

The reviewer found two steps in the wrong order and filed a short note in the record repository instead of editing the checklist directly. The author read the note and decided to keep the original order for one step and swap the other. [ev: MSG-20250106-091500-01, notes/2025-01-06-review.md]

## Why it worked

Because the reviewer only filed notes, the author could see every change before it happened, and the record shows who suggested what. [ev: notes/2025-01-06-review.md]

> **In one sentence:** A reviewer that files notes instead of editing keeps the author in charge.

**Try this week:**

1. Ask one assistant to draft and another to review.
2. Keep the review as a separate note.
3. Decide each point yourself.
